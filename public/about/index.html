A little context

Hi, I'm Muhammad.

I like turning practical ideas into software people can actually use.

I'm an independent developer who builds digital products and practical tools. CashNote came from wanting a straightforward place to record income and expenses, keep budgets visible, save repeat transactions and review financial activity without depending on a cloud account or connecting a bank.

The product has grown from a focused income-and-expense tracker into a fuller personal finance workspace: reusable templates, budgets, charts, PDF and CSV reports, activity history, multiple local profiles and an optional AI assistant. CashNote AI can ask follow-up questions when a request is incomplete, then prepare a proposal that you must confirm. The product is developed independently, so it keeps evolving through real use, testing and feedback.

Privacy & local storage

Your workspace stays
on your computer.

CashNote is designed around local profiles and local financial records. Current Windows releases store new data beneath %APPDATA%\CashNote. You do not need a CashNote cloud account, bank login or financial-service connection to enter transactions manually.

Each local profile has its own username and PIN. The PIN is a local access lock, not an online identity service and not a guarantee that the stored financial files are encrypted.

✓ Separate local profiles✓ Manual backup export✓ CashNote-branded storage✓ No bank connection required
⌂A clear privacy note

Local storage is not the same as encryption.

In the current release, financial data is stored locally and is not encrypted at rest. The local PIN is an application-level access lock, not Windows disk encryption. Protect your Windows user account, keep your device updated, and export backups to a location you control. Older Nivora-branded data is migrated to the CashNote storage location by the desktop app; keep a backup before upgrading.

The website's download total is read from public GitHub Release asset metadata. It is an aggregate count, not a per-visitor download history. GitHub processes installer downloads under its own terms.
AI · YOUR CHOICE

Local by default.
Connected only when you ask.

CashNote AI is optional and is not required for normal finance tracking. You enter your own API key for a supported OpenAI-compatible provider and need an internet connection to use it.

✦

What is sent when you use AI?

Your message and the relevant finance context needed for that request are sent to the AI provider you selected. The provider processes that data under its own privacy terms and may charge for API usage.

What stays in your hands?

AI suggestions are not applied silently. CashNote asks for missing details, displays a review proposal and requires your confirmation before a supported change is saved. You can use the app without configuring AI.
